# Q3 Financial Report (Finance)

Confidential finance document. Access: finance team and org admins.

## Revenue

Q3 revenue was $4.2M, up 18% quarter over quarter and 42% year over year.
Subscription revenue contributed $3.6M and professional services $0.6M.
Net revenue retention for the quarter was 117%.

## Costs

Total operating expenses in Q3 were $3.1M. The largest line items were
payroll at $2.2M, cloud infrastructure at $410K, and sales commissions at
$260K.

## Margin

Gross margin held at 78%. Operating margin improved to 26% from 21% in Q2,
driven by infrastructure cost reductions from the reserved-instance program.

## Outlook

The finance team forecasts Q4 revenue between $4.6M and $4.9M, assuming the
two enterprise deals in late-stage negotiation close before mid-November.
