# Budget Plan 2026 (Finance)

Confidential finance document. Access: finance team and org admins.

## Headcount budget

The 2026 plan funds 14 new hires: eight in engineering, three in sales, two
in customer success, and one in finance. Total additional payroll cost is
$2.4M annualized.

## Infrastructure budget

Cloud spend is budgeted at $1.9M for 2026, a 12% increase over 2025. The
reserved-instance program is expected to offset half of the growth from the
Kafka cluster expansion.

## Marketing budget

Marketing receives $850K for 2026, split 60/40 between demand generation and
brand. Conference sponsorships are capped at $120K.

## Contingency

A 7% contingency reserve of $520K covers currency movements and unplanned
tooling costs. Drawdowns above $50K require CFO approval.
