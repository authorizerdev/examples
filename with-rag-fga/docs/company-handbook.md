# Acme Company Handbook

Welcome to Acme. This handbook is public to every employee.

## Working hours

Acme operates on flexible working hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to
15:00 in your local timezone. Teams agree on overlap windows during quarterly
planning.

## Time off

Every employee has 25 days of paid vacation per year plus public holidays.
Vacation requests go through the HR portal and are approved by your manager
within two business days.

## Communication

Company-wide announcements happen in the #general channel every Monday.
Engineering, finance, and HR each maintain their own team spaces with
restricted access for sensitive material.

## Expenses

Business expenses under $100 do not require pre-approval. Submit receipts
through the expense tool within 30 days of purchase.
