# Performance Review Process (HR)

Confidential HR document. Access: HR team and org admins.

## Cycle

Performance reviews run twice a year, in June and December. Each cycle takes
four weeks: self review, peer feedback, manager assessment, and calibration.

## Peer feedback

Every employee nominates three to five peers. Managers may add reviewers for
cross-team work. Feedback is structured around impact, collaboration, and
craft.

## Calibration

Calibration sessions group managers by department. Ratings follow a five-point
scale and are validated against written evidence, not recency or visibility.

## Outcomes

Review outcomes feed the June compensation cycle and promotion decisions.
Employees receive written summaries within one week of calibration closing.
