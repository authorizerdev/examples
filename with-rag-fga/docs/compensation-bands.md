# Compensation Bands (HR)

Confidential HR document. Access: HR team and org admins.

## Engineering bands

Engineering L3 pays $95K to $120K base, L4 pays $120K to $155K, L5 pays
$155K to $195K, and L6 (staff) pays $195K to $240K. Equity refreshers are
granted at each promotion.

## Sales bands

Account executives have a $80K to $110K base with a 50/50 on-target split.
Sales engineers are banded $105K to $140K base.

## Geographic adjustment

Compensation uses three geographic tiers. Tier 1 (SF, NYC, London) pays the
full band, tier 2 pays 90%, and tier 3 pays 80%. Tier assignment follows the
employee's tax residence.

## Review cadence

Bands are benchmarked against market data every April and adjusted in the
June compensation cycle.
